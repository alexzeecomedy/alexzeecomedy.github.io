9/13/23 - What a Show! presents comedian Dan Alten feat. Ashlynn Lutz 9/16/23 Madison, AL

Nationally touring comedian Dan Alten struts his stuff at Boxcar Bar and Grill in Madison, AL on Saturday September 16th starting at 7:30 PM! Featuring beloved Huntsville comedian Ashlynn Lutz!

Dan Alten is a nationally touring comedian from Kentucky who has headlined shows in 30 states playing everything from theaters to punk basements to art galleries to beloved comedy clubs.

He has opened for Kyle Kinane, Rory Scovel, Sarah Sherman from SNL, Johnny Pemberton, Hari Kondabolu, rapper Open Mike Eagle, DJ Doug Pound & David Leibe Hart from Tim & Eric, various punk bands with silly names, and a guy at an open mic who tried to throw his father’s ashes into the crowd.

He has recorded 2 albums, written a zine, and filmed a pilot for an Amazon Prime stand up show that has still not aired. You will enjoy him and if not you can spit on his car.

Ashlynn Lutz is a beloved local comic commonly referred to as "Huntsville's Darling."

What a Joke comedy is Jaylen Brown and Alex Zee.
