10/31/23 - Fusion Barbecue Comedy Open Mic EVERY Wednesday in Huntsville, Alabama!

Every week the funniest comedians from around Huntsville and Madison, Alabama and beyond gather at Fusion Barbecue to work on new material. Hosted by Jaylen Brown and Alex Zee. Come see a different line up of live comedy every week or try it out for yourself and sign up! Sign ups start at 8 PM, show starts at 8:30 PM.
