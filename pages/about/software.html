My favorite operating system I've ever used. Inspired by Privacy Guides, Techlore, and Mutahar from Some Ordinary Gamers to make the switch to Linux in mid 2025. The move has been so immensely freeing.

DaVinci Resolve Studio

The best video editing program I've used hands down. I've used free and/or open-source programs all my life until I started editing the Clown College Podcast when we started using Premier Pro. After a year of that, a few months of DaVinci had us hooked and we don't look back.
