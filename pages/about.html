About

Alex Zee is a stand-up comedian based in Huntsville, AL.

Born in Baltimore, MD, Alex grew up moving across the midwest garnering the favor of whites. When he was 18, he left his family home for the restless life of a comedian.

Since then, he has performed all over the country, most recently on Epic Comedy Hour in Huntsville, Alabama Don't Tell Comedy in Huntsville, Alabama, and Keenan Thompson's Comedy Competition at the Stardome in Birmingham, Alabama.

He has also been interviewed by Katy Ganaway for Huntsville's NPR station, WLRH 89.3, on Arts Underground.

He hosts a weekly open mic on Wednesdays at Phat Sammy's in downtown Huntsville, AL with Branden Willis where comedians from all over the state and country drop in to work their material out.
