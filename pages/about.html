Bio

The story so far...

Early Life

Alex Zee is a stand-up comedian based in Huntsville, AL.

Born in Baltimore, MD, when the atmosphere was around 361.93 ppm CO2, 81.9 ppm higher than preindustrial levels. Alex grew up moving across the Midwest garnering the favor of whites. When he was 18, he left his family home for the restless life of a comedian.

Past Performances

Since then, he has performed all over the country, most recently on Epic Comedy Hour in Huntsville, Alabama, Don't Tell Comedy in Huntsville, Alabama, and The X5 Podcast Comedy Competition at the Stardome in Birmingham, Alabama.

Media Appearances

He has also been interviewed by Katy Ganaway for Huntsville's NPR station, WLRH 89.3, on Arts Underground.

Phat Sammy's Phusion Comedy Open Mic in Huntsville, Alabama

He hosts a weekly comedy open mic on Wednesdays at Phat Sammy's in downtown Huntsville, AL with Branden Willis of the Clown College Podcast where comedians from all over the state and country drop in to work their material out.
