About

Alex Zee is a stand-up comedian based in Huntsville, AL.

Born in Baltimore, MD, Alex grew up moving across the midwest garnering the favor of whites. When he was 18, he left his family home for the restless life of a comedian.

Since then, he has performed all over the country but most recently could have been seen on You Look Like in Huntsville, AL, BrewHaHa in Tuscaloosa, AL, and The Laugh Garden in Birmingham, AL.

He hosts a weekly open mic on Sundays at Boxcar Bar and Grill in Madison, AL with Jaylen Brown where comedians from all over the state and country drop in to work their material out.
